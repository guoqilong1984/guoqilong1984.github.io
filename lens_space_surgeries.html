The Bleiler–Litherland Conjecture on Lens Space Surgeries

Qilong Guo

Preprint, 2026

Abstract

It is a longstanding conjecture of Bleiler and Litherland that if surgery on a hyperbolic knot K in S3 yields a lens space L(r,s), then r ≥ 18. Baker reduced the conjecture to excluding a hyperbolic knot K satisfying g(K) = 4, 14-surgery on K yielding L(14,11), and ΔK(t) = ΔT(3,5)(t). We rule out this remaining case, proving the conjecture. As a corollary, 14 is a characterizing slope for T(3,5).
