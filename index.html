The Involution Rank of Mapping Class Groups of Closed Surfaces

Qilong Guo and Chunxing Yan

We determine the involution rank of the mapping class groups of closed orientable surfaces. In particular, the involution rank is 4 in genus 3 and 3 for every genus at least 4.

The Bleiler–Litherland Conjecture on Lens Space Surgeries

Qilong Guo

We prove the Bleiler–Litherland conjecture that every lens space obtained by surgery on a hyperbolic knot in S^3 has order at least 18. As a corollary, 14 is a characterizing slope for T(3,5).
